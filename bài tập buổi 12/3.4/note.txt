4.4. Yeu cau 3.4 - Kiem tra DNS

Muc tieu: xac dinh DNS server, kiem tra phan giai ten mien,
phan biet loi DNS voi loi mat ket noi mang.

Lenh da dung:
  resolvectl status
    -> xac dinh DNS server dang su dung
  dig google.com +short
    -> kiem tra phan giai ten mien
  ping -c 2 8.8.8.8
    -> kiem tra ket noi mang (khong qua DNS)
  ping -c 2 google.com
    -> kiem tra ket noi mang + DNS
  dig @192.0.2.1 google.com +short +time=2 +tries=1
    -> gia lap loi DNS (IP 192.0.2.1 thuoc dai thu nghiem,
       khong co DNS server that) - khong sua cau hinh he thong

Ket qua:
  - DNS server: [dien gia tri tu resolvectl]
  - Phan giai google.com -> 142.250.197.46 (thanh cong)
  - ping 8.8.8.8: 0% packet loss
  - ping google.com: 0% packet loss
  - dig toi DNS gia: timed out, no servers could be reached

Ket luan:
  Khi ping IP thanh cong nhung phan giai ten mien that bai
  -> loi nam o DNS, khong phai mat ket noi mang.
